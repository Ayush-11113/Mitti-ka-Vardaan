Mitti Ka Vardaan
Story Impact Participate

Student-led composting initiative

Every movement begins in the soil.

Born from Ansh Gulati's Republic Day address on January 26, 2025, Mitti Ka Vardaan turns everyday organic waste into living proof of change.

Seed

A small beginning

The idea starts quietly: one address, one call to care for soil, one invitation for a community to participate.

What changed

Waste became visible. Soil became personal.

01

Hands-on workshops

Students and residents learned the why behind composting, not just the steps.

02

Working composting unit

A visible, society-owned system made the result tangible and repeatable.

03

Community participation

Families began sorting at source and caring for saplings as a shared habit.

The model

Seed, soil, roots, care, community, future.

Mitti Ka Vardaan is designed as a practical cycle: teach the habit, show the compost, nurture the plant, then help another community repeat it.

  1. SeedStart with awareness.
  2. SoilSeparate organic waste.
  3. RootsBring residents together.
  4. CareMaintain compost and saplings.
  5. ScaleRepeat the program with new groups.
